Let’s connect the crystal ball

  1. Turn it off. Hold the middle button for 3 seconds.
  2. Plug it into this Mac with a USB data cable.
  3. Hold the middle button until a USB picture shows on the ball.

Nothing happening? Some cables only charge. Try another one, or a port right on the Mac.
